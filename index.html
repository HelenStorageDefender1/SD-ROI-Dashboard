Hours by Associated PM

Total time logged (dev + QA combined) per feature, grouped by the Associate PM field on the SD board. "Unassigned" covers parent epics that sit above the roadmap-tagged cards and haven't had an Associate PM set directly. "# of Facilities/Orgs" is pulled from BigQuery per feature (feature toggles, PMS type, or usage signals depending on the feature) and excludes internal, test, staging, and demo facilities. "N/A" means the feature has no facility-level concept (e.g. an internal automation task); "—" means the mapping hasn't been provided yet.

Feature Value Breakdown

Value score = facilities/orgs using the feature ÷ (hours spent building it × days it's been live in production, per Confluence release notes). This rewards features reaching facilities quickly relative to both effort AND time — a feature live 2 weeks with strong adoption outranks one live 6 months with weak adoption. Each feature's % share is that score as a portion of the total across all measured features, bucketed as Low (<3%) / Medium (3–10%) / High (10–20%) / Extensive (≥20%). Still not a dollar ROI — no revenue or cost figures involved. Donut shows only Medium/High/Extensive features, re-normalized to 100%.
